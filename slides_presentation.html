Key Points
  • Primary Keys: id auto-increment unique integer in all 6 entities.
  • Foreign Keys: firm_id, society_id, member_id enforce strict referential integrity.
  • ACID Transactions: Financial receipts and batch maintenance billing execute atomically in SQLite.
  • Password Masking: Passwords in FIRM table are masked (••••••••) across all views.
